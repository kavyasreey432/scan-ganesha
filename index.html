About Lord Ganesha

Story, Form, Symbolism, Worship and Vinayaka Chaturthi

Lord Ganesha

Lord Vinayaka, also known as Ganesha or Ganapati, is the widely worshipped elephant-headed deity in Hinduism. He is revered as the lord of wisdom, prosperity, good beginnings and the remover of obstacles.

Devotees traditionally invoke Lord Ganesha before beginning important activities such as a new venture, journey, examination, ceremony or ritual.

Birth and Form

Creation of Vinayaka

According to a popular traditional account, Goddess Parvati created Vinayaka to guard the entrance while she prepared to bathe.

The Elephant Head

When Lord Shiva returned, Vinayaka stopped him from entering. After the confrontation, Shiva restored Vinayaka with the head of an elephant.

Symbolism of Lord Ganesha

👂

Large Ears

They remind us to listen carefully to wisdom and good advice.

🐘

Elephant Head

Represents wisdom, strength, intelligence and memory.

🦷

Single Tusk

Symbolically associated with retaining what is good.

🍚

Large Belly

Represents accepting and digesting life's experiences.

🐭

Mouse Vehicle

Symbolically associated with controlling restless desires.

🪷

Lotus

Represents purity and spiritual growth.

Ganesha Chaturthi

Ganesha Chaturthi, also known as Vinayaka Chaturthi, celebrates the birth of Lord Ganesha. Homes and public spaces are decorated, prayers are offered and communities come together in celebration.

Ganesha & Computer Science

Connecting traditional wisdom with modern technology.

🧠

Wisdom & Intelligence

Computer Science requires logical thinking, reasoning and problem solving.

Think → Analyse → Choose → Solve. Strong computing begins with clear thinking.
🐘

Removing Obstacles

Programmers identify and remove bugs just as Ganesha is traditionally associated with removing obstacles.

Debugging means finding an obstacle, understanding it and fixing it.
💡

Innovation

Technology depends on creativity, experimentation and improvement.

Good engineers experiment, learn from failure and improve solutions.
🤖

Artificial Intelligence

AI uses data, patterns and computation to solve problems.

AI learns patterns from data, while humans provide judgement, ethics and responsibility.
