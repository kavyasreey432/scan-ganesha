About Lord Ganesha

The Remover of Obstacles

Lord Ganesha is one of the most beloved deities in Hindu tradition. He is associated with wisdom, knowledge, new beginnings and the removal of obstacles.

He is traditionally worshipped before beginning important activities.

👂 Large Ears

Listen carefully and learn from everything around you.

🐘 Elephant Head

Represents wisdom, intelligence and understanding.

🍚 Large Belly

Symbolizes the ability to absorb experiences.

🐭 Mouse

Represents control over restless desires.

🦷 Broken Tusk

Symbolizes sacrifice, determination and knowledge.

🌺 Modak

Represents the sweetness of spiritual knowledge.

💻 Ganesha & Computer Science

🧠

Intelligence

Ganesha represents wisdom and intelligence. Computer Science also depends on logical thinking and problem solving.

Algorithms solve problems step by step, just as wisdom helps overcome obstacles.
🔐

Elephant Head

The elephant is associated with memory and intelligence.

Computers store information in memory so programs can use it later.
🐭

Small Mouse

Ganesha's mouse vehicle is often associated with restless desires.

Small components can perform important tasks in a large system.

Removing Obstacles
